# Known sign-language data sources

Updated 2026-10-17. This is the project's discovery catalog, not a blanket rights clearance. The project owner reports counsel's advice that UMI's public model track may use How2Sign and other noncommercial datasets to train an open-weight model; counsel treats Bittensor miner grants as grants for that open-source work. This handling applies to the public model track. Review each source's exact version and terms before download, and record the applicable counsel decision, attribution, access conditions and transform history in the experiment ledger. Permission-gated access and third-party source-video or redistribution terms require separate review.

### Local raw-clip integrity quarantine

An annotation-only duration audit on October 14, 2026 found six of the 4,928 local How2Sign training bindings severely truncated: actual duration is less than half the publisher's realigned interval. One contains a single frame for a 3.30-second sentence, including in its original aligned source. Keep all source files and historical experiments intact, but exclude these six identities from new raw-video training until the source video is recovered and re-audited. The complete finding list, pinned annotation/source hashes and selection rule are in `runs/tiny-rgb-duration-integrity-v1/receipt.json`, SHA-256 `a9e2eadb88a8f3f8363ec67cb65f3d6c69c0ee794c68a94e85d73e50e50ac756`. Future raw training currently has 7,523 eligible bindings across How2Sign, FLEURS, ASL STEM and OpenASL. This integrity check uses no model scores and does not establish that the remaining labels are semantically aligned or explain all previous regressions.

## ASL isolated signs, lexicons and linguistic annotations

The owner's October 16 word-to-phrase curriculum uses complete source clips with single-English-word captions, not verified one-sign annotations. The metadata audit found 1,956 candidates at 0.75-8 seconds; its 60-label pilot selected 228 training and 96 signer-disjoint development clips. All 324 originals were acquired on October 17 after physical headroom passed the unchanged 80 GiB guard, totaling 277,445,367 bytes. Every original has a source-member hash and complete decoded-frame/duration receipt. A byte and low-resolution cross-role re-encode screen found no duplicate flags; that screen does not exclude every possible transformed or overlapping video. Complete 25 fps derivatives preserve source dimensions and intervals, totaling 35,245 frames and 361,884,409 bytes. The original role records stay intact; applying the later P0/P19 ROI diagnostic exclusions leaves 214 potential ROI-lineage training clips across all 60 labels, with at least two training signers per label. Preserve all eight development signers across later local curriculum training, exclude P0/P19 when retaining those ROI diagnostics, and initialize a word arm from original public weights because historical local weights may have seen the newly reserved signers. Keep source videos and derivatives private under the existing ASL-STEM research license. See `runs/terra-word-data-v1/plan.json`, `runs/terra-word-data-v1/raw-audit.json`, `runs/terra-word-normalization-v1/complete.json` and the original `runs/terra-word-curriculum-capacity-v1/metadata-only.json`.

On October 17, the complete word-pilot landmark audit verified all 324 sidecars and their original roles, covering all 35,245 normalized frames in 250,606,754 bytes. Missing detections are retained. The audit is `runs/terra-word-landmarks-v1/complete-audit.json`, SHA-256 `05f2ce4f71fb0eca0a913747ed97ef8fbe6063b522cef506eff67ca42b61c333`. This audit establishes sidecar integrity; native-input, model and semantic checks are recorded separately below.

The native112px pixel/pose preflight also completed on all 310 eligible inputs: 214 training clips with 12,147 sampled frames and 96 development clips with 4,828. It checked every crop against a separate native decode and retained all missing-detection behavior. All per-clip receipt hashes and complete shapes were independently verified; the three metadata-selected training proof clips reproduced their RGB, pose and timeline hashes on reread. See `checkpoints/terra-word-native112-data-v1/data.json` and `runs/terra-word-native112-data-v1/verified-inputs.json`. This establishes input integrity separately from model qualification and held-out semantic quality.

The actual full-model word Terra-risk path passed CPU/Metal qualification on October 17 before a bounded word-training run launched at 18:45:59 UTC. Its frozen recipe starts from original public C5 values and schedules four complete passes over all 214 word clips, with all 350 functional visual tensors trainable. The original sequence/language components and unused mask tokens remain frozen. Qualification rewards are training evidence; no trained-word held-out quality result or promotion is established by this launch. See `runs/c5-word-semantic-gradient-v1/terminal-audit.json`, `runs/c5-word-semantic-training-v1/plan.json` and the current ledger in `STUDIO_TRAINING.md`.
